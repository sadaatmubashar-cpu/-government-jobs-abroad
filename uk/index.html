Government Jobs in the UK for Foreigners

The UK hires foreign workers into its public sector at real scale — above all the NHS — but only through licensed sponsors and eligible occupations. This hub covers the three official portals, how sponsorship actually works, and the 2025 rule change that closed the care-worker route to applicants abroad.

Which UK public-sector portals hire foreign workers?

NHS Jobs carries health roles across England, many with Health and Care Worker visa sponsorship — the largest single route on this site. Civil Service Jobs covers central government, where nationality rules apply per advert and reserved posts do not sponsor. Find a Job, the Department for Work and Pensions service, covers the wider public sector, including schools and local government.

Sponsorship: the Home Office and the Register of Licensed Sponsors

Only employers licensed by the UK Home Office can sponsor a Skilled Worker or Health and Care Worker visa. Before trusting any offer, find the employer on the Register of Licensed Sponsors — if it is not listed, it cannot sponsor you. The role must also meet the visa's salary threshold, unless it appears on the Immigration Salary List of shortage occupations. A genuine sponsor never charges you for your Certificate of Sponsorship.

Can care workers still be sponsored from outside the UK?

Overseas recruitment of care workers and senior care workers (occupation codes 6135 and 6136) closed on 22 July 2025. UK care providers can no longer sponsor new care workers from outside the UK. People already in the UK on that route may extend or switch under transitional rules until 22 July 2028. This closure does not affect nurses, doctors or allied health professionals — overseas recruitment for those roles remains open. Any advert still offering UK care-worker sponsorship to applicants abroad is out of date or a scam.

Registration and language tests

Regulated professionals register before an employer can hire them: nurses with the NMC, doctors with the GMC, allied health professionals with the HCPC, and teachers normally need Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) recognition. English-language evidence usually means IELTS or the OET for health roles. Start registration early — it takes months.
