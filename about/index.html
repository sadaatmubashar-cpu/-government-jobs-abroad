About Government Jobs Abroad

Government Jobs Abroad is an independent guide that helps international workers find government-job routes in the USA, Canada, the United Kingdom and Europe. We explain which routes are genuinely open to foreign workers, which are closed — and we link only to official government portals, where every real vacancy lives.

What this site is

Finding a government job in another country is confusing for one simple reason: most government jobs are reserved for that country's own citizens, and the exceptions are scattered across dozens of official websites, each with its own rules. This site organises those exceptions honestly. We cover four destinations — the USA, Canada, the UK and Europe — and sort every route by eligibility first, so you never waste time applying for a job you cannot legally hold.

We are a guide, not a job board and not a recruitment agency. We do not copy vacancy listings from official portals, and the one live snapshot we show — recent USA federal jobs from USAJOBS — is clearly labelled with its source and its citizenship warning.

What you will find here

  • 18 job pathways across the USA, Canada, the UK and Europe, each tagged by eligibility — open to non-citizens, sponsorship or work-permit possible, limited exceptions, or citizens only — and each linked to its official portal.
  • 8 official government portals explained: USAJOBS (USA), Job Bank Canada and GC Jobs (Canada), Civil Service Jobs, NHS Jobs and Find a Job (UK), and EURES and EU Careers / EPSO (Europe).
  • Country guides covering each destination's portals, citizenship rules and visa or work-permit routes.
  • Country hub pages for the USA, Canada, the UK and Europe — one page per destination, gathering its pathways, official portals and the rules that decide who can apply.
  • An eligibility checker that matches your destination, status and field to the routes realistically open to you.
  • Application guides — the US federal resume, the UK supporting statement, and how government interviews are scored.
  • A blog of in-depth guides on nursing abroad, UK visa sponsorship, Canada's Job Bank, and which government jobs are honestly open without citizenship.

An honest note about US federal jobs

Most US federal jobs legally require US citizenship. Green-card holders are not eligible for most federal posts, and the US government does not sponsor work visas for civil-service jobs. The main exception is some healthcare hiring — notably the Department of Veterans Affairs — where a non-citizen may be appointed only when no qualified US citizen is available. We say this plainly on every USA pathway and on the USA jobs snapshot, because pretending otherwise would waste your time. The routes genuinely open to international workers are mainly public employers — health systems, schools, hospitals and municipalities in Canada, the UK and Europe — where a genuine job offer comes first and a visa or work permit follows.

How we check our information

Our guides are written from official sources — the government portals and immigration pages linked throughout the site — and each blog article carries source notes naming them. Vacancies, deadlines, salaries and eligibility rules change, so always confirm the details on the official portal before you apply. If you spot something on this site that looks out of date, please tell us — corrections are welcome on our contact page.
