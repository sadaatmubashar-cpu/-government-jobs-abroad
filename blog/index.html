Frequently asked questions about this blog

What topics do the guides on this blog cover?
The blog publishes in-depth guides for international workers: nursing jobs abroad, UK visa sponsorship, jobs in Canada for foreigners, explainers for the eight official government portals, and which government jobs are honestly open without citizenship.

Who writes the guides on this blog?
Every guide is written by Hamid Aziz, site owner and editor. He is not an immigration adviser or a recruiter — each article is checked against the official sources named in its source notes, so you can verify every rule yourself before you apply.

Can I apply for a job through this blog?
No. The blog is a guide — every article links to the official government portal where the real vacancy lives, and applying on those portals is always free. This site takes no applications and charges no fee.
