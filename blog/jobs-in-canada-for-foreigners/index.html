Jobs in Canada for Foreigners: Job Bank & Work Permits

Author: Hamid Aziz, site owner & editor About the author: Hamid Aziz is the owner and editor of Government Jobs Abroad. He is not an immigration adviser or a recruiter — every rule in this guide is taken from the official sources linked on the page, so you can check each one yourself before you apply. Published: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 23 November 2026 Sources checked against Job Bank Canada and Canada.ca — see the source notes at the end.

Canada is the most realistic of the four destinations on this site for foreign workers — but the route is employer-led, not government-led. You get hired by an employer first; the work permit follows. This guide shows how Job Bank hiring works, where the Global Talent Stream helps, and which fields hire internationally most often.

Start on Job Bank — Canada's official job board

Job Bank Canada is the federal government's national job board. Provincial health authorities, school districts, municipalities and private employers all post there. Anyone can browse it for free, from anywhere.

What matters is the individual posting: does the employer consider international candidates, and will they support a work permit? Apply exactly as the posting instructs, and keep expectations honest — a posting that requires existing work authorization will not sponsor you, whatever an agent claims.

See the three Canada routes side by side in the pathways list and the Canada country guide.

The work-permit routes that matter

Employer-supported work permit (LMIA route)

For many roles, the employer first obtains a Labour Market Impact Assessment showing the job can be offered to a foreign worker, then you apply for the work permit. The job offer comes first — and it is illegal for anyone to charge you, the worker, for a job offer or an LMIA.

Global Talent Stream

For in-demand tech and specialist roles, the Global Talent Stream gives eligible employers a faster work-permit route. It is employer-driven: you cannot apply for it on your own, and it does not bypass the need for a genuine job offer.

Permanent residence is a separate system

Express Entry is a points-based permanent-residence system run on Canada.ca. It is not a job board, and a job offer is only one possible part of a profile. Do not confuse an immigration consultant's Express Entry pitch with an actual job offer.

The fields that hire foreigners most

Healthcare

The strongest route. Provincial health employers recruit nurses and care workers internationally, but licensing is provincial — credential assessment and registration with the destination province's regulator come first. Detail: Nursing Jobs Abroad.

IT and engineering

Tech employers use Job Bank and the Global Talent Stream; regulated engineering roles need provincial licensing. Confirm the posting welcomes international candidates before applying.

Teaching

School districts hire through Job Bank, with demand in rural areas, French immersion and specialist subjects. Provincial teacher certification is required, and assessment happens before most districts will interview.

GC Jobs: the honest exception

The federal public service portal, GC Jobs, gives legal preference to veterans, Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Most external postings are effectively closed to work-permit holders. Your practical door into Canadian public employment is an employer — a health authority, district or municipality — not the federal service. More context: Government Jobs for Foreigners Without Citizenship.

Your step-by-step

  1. Run the eligibility checker with Canada selected.
  2. Start credential assessment if your field is regulated.
  3. Search Job Bank by occupation and province; target postings open to international candidates.
  4. Apply to the employer directly, exactly as instructed.
  5. With a genuine offer, follow the official work-permit process on Canada.ca — and pay no one for the offer itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can a foreigner get a job in Canada?

Get hired by an employer first; the work permit follows. Search Job Bank Canada — the official federal job board — for postings open to international candidates, apply exactly as the posting instructs, and with a genuine job offer follow the official work-permit process on Canada.ca.

What is an LMIA, and do I have to pay for it?

An LMIA (Labour Market Impact Assessment) is the employer's step showing that a job can be offered to a foreign worker, before you apply for a work permit. It is illegal for anyone to charge you, the worker, for a job offer or an LMIA.

What is Canada's Global Talent Stream?

A faster work-permit route for in-demand tech and specialist roles. It is employer-driven — you cannot apply for it on your own — and it does not bypass the need for a genuine job offer from an eligible employer.

Can foreigners apply on GC Jobs, Canada's federal public service portal?

Mostly no. GC Jobs gives legal preference to veterans, Canadian citizens and permanent residents, so most external postings are effectively closed to work-permit holders. The practical door into Canadian public employment is an employer — a health authority, school district or municipality — hiring through Job Bank.

Is Express Entry a way to get a job in Canada?

No. Express Entry is a points-based permanent-residence system run on Canada.ca, not a job board, and a job offer is only one possible part of a profile. Do not confuse an immigration consultant's Express Entry pitch with an actual job offer.
