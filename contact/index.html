Contact Government Jobs Abroad

The way to reach us is email. Write to us with questions about the guides on this site, corrections, or feedback — we read every message.

Email us support@toolmater.com

Tap the address to open your email app, or copy it into any email service. This is the only contact address for this site.

Write to support@toolmater.com

What can I email you about?

  • Questions about a pathway, a country guide or an article on this site that is not already answered in the FAQ.
  • Corrections — a rule that has changed, a portal link that no longer works, or anything that looks out of date. Please include the page address and, if you can, the official page that shows the change.
  • Feedback — what helped, what was confusing, and which country or profession you would like a guide on next.

What we cannot do — please read this first

  • We never take job applications. This site is a guide only. We cannot accept applications or CVs, forward them to an employer, or check the status of an application. Every application is made on the official government portal linked from each pathway — USAJOBS, Job Bank, GC Jobs, Civil Service Jobs, NHS Jobs, Find a Job or EURES.
  • We never charge a fee — not for applications, not for advice, not for anything. If anyone asks you to pay for a government job, a work permit, an LMIA or a Certificate of Sponsorship — including anyone claiming to be us — it is a scam.
  • We cannot give personal immigration advice or tell you whether a specific offer you received is genuine. The check that works: find the same vacancy on the official portal. If it is not there, do not pay anything and do not hand over documents.

For your own safety, please do not email us copies of your passport, certificates or other personal documents — we never need them, and no genuine employer asks for them by email either.

What should I check before I write?

Many questions are already answered on the site: run the eligibility checker for your destination and field, browse the 18 pathways, or check the frequently asked questions. If your answer is not there, email support@toolmater.com and we will help if we can.

Remember: Government Jobs Abroad never takes job applications and never charges a fee. Applications happen only on the official government websites linked from this site, and those services are free.
